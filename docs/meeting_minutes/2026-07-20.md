## Discussion

### 1. Problem Formulation

The team agreed to formulate the task as a **Top-K recommendation problem**.

- **Input:** Soil and environmental features.
- **Output:** A ranked Top-K list of recommended crop classes.

---

### 2. Model Selection

The following models were selected for comparison:

- **Baseline:** Exact class-wise KNN.
- **Proposed Method:** KNN with K-Means-based search optimization.

The objective is to compare the recommendation quality and inference efficiency before and after applying the search optimization strategy.

---

### 3. Distance Metrics

The recommendation models will be evaluated using three similarity/distance measures:

- Euclidean Distance
- Manhattan Distance
- Cosine Similarity

---

### 4. Evaluation Metrics

The team agreed to evaluate recommendation performance using:

- Precision@K
- Recall@K
- MAP@K

Optional metrics:

- NDCG@K (ranking quality evaluation for recommendation systems)
